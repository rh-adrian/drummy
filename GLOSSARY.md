# Drummy

An AI drummer you talk to. The chat AI writes grooves; drummy plays them through real drum samples.

## Language

**Groove**:
A short drum pattern (one to four bars) that drummy loops until it is changed or stopped.
_Avoid_: Beat, drum track

**Tab**:
Someone's written drum transcription of a specific song, posted on a tab site.
_Avoid_: Tabs (as a singular), drum chart

**Listing**:
One tab site's page for a tab, with that site's rating and vote count. The same tab posted on two sites is two listings, each rated on its own.
_Avoid_: Version, tab page

**Song groove**:
A groove built from a tab for one section of one specific song, identified by artist and title.
_Avoid_: Song beat, saved beat, drum track

**Section**:
A named part of a song, such as intro, verse, chorus or bridge. Every song in the song library has a main section: the groove the song is best known for.
_Avoid_: Part, segment

**Song library**:
The song grooves saved on the user's own machine, for instant recall.
_Avoid_: Cache, saved beats
